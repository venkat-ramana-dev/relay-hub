SUMMARY

| VUs | Requests | RPS   | P50      | P95      | Error % |

| 10  | 13,169   | 54.74 | 33.71 ms | 61.95 ms | 0.35%   |

OBSERVATIONS
============

• Throughput remained approximately 45–55 RPS as concurrency increased
  from 10 to 30 VUs.

• P95 latency remained below approximately 80 ms for the normal request
  population.

• Error rate increased with concurrency:
  0.35% → 0.61% → 0.93%.

• Local machine CPU initially became heavily utilized during load
  generation, while memory remained relatively high.

• Some requests experienced very high latency/timeouts, significantly
  increasing average latency.

• These results represent a local development-machine benchmark and
  should not be interpreted as the absolute capacity of RelayHub.

• Further investigation is required to identify whether the limiting
  resource is the application, database, JVM, connection pool, Docker,
  or local machine.
